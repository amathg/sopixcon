N2

In-depth diagnosis & BGA / SMD repair

Intermediate level — restoring faulty equipment

Level N2 is for equipment with a functional fault or failed electronic components. It involves advanced electronic diagnosis and component-level repair to restore the equipment's original performance.

Objective

Repair, not just replace

N2 goes beyond a simple functional check: the aim is to pinpoint the cause of a fault at circuit level, then work directly on the components concerned — replacing surface-mount devices (SMD) or repairing BGA chips — rather than replacing the entire board or unit. This approach recovers value from equipment that would otherwise be scrapped.

Process steps

A 5-step protocol

In-depth electronic diagnosis

Testing on specialised benches, electrical measurements, thermal analysis and signal checks to pinpoint the source of the fault.

Identification of failed components

Isolating the component(s) at fault (capacitors, integrated circuits, connectors, BGA chips) and assessing whether repair is feasible.

SMD component replacement

Desoldering and refitting surface-mount components using precision micro-soldering stations, under the microscope.

BGA repair

Reballing and reattaching BGA (Ball Grid Array) chips on a rework station, with controlled thermal profiles to preserve the integrity of the board.

Validation testing & enhanced quality control

Full post-repair functional check, load and stability testing, then detailed documentation of the work carried out.

Damaged circuit boards

Intermittent faults

Occasional malfunctions requiring precise electronic diagnosis.

Telecom & industrial equipment

Equipment that would be costly or hard to replace entirely.

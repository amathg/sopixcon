Absolute confidentiality

Commitment 1 / 6

Non-disclosure agreements signed with every client, to protect your technical and commercial information throughout our collaboration.

What this means in practice

  • A non-disclosure agreement (NDA) signed before any site visit or exchange of sensitive data
  • Access restricted to the teams assigned to your project
  • Data destroyed or returned on simple request at the end of the project

Why it matters to you

  • Your technical and commercial data stays protected
  • You stay in control of how your information is used
  • A relationship of trust from the very first contact

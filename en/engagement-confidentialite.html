Commitment

Your information stays yours

In the telecom industry, your technical and commercial data is a strategic asset. Sopixcon signs a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) with every client, before any site visit or exchange of sensitive data.

The company also guarantees its commercial independence: two separate entities, with no cross-over of information between competing clients. Every team member signs an individual confidentiality undertaking.

What this means in practice

  • A non-disclosure agreement (NDA) signed before any site visit or exchange of sensitive data
  • Access restricted to the teams assigned to your project
  • Data destroyed or returned on simple request at the end of the project

Why it matters to you

  • Your technical and commercial data stays protected
  • You stay in control of how your information is used
  • A relationship of trust from the very first contact

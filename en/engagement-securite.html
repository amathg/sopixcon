Data security

Commitment 3 / 6

Storage media are wiped or destroyed before any dismantling — an essential guarantee in sectors where data is critical.

Commitment

No data leaves the country

Sopixcon never accesses the content of storage media. Repairable equipment is reset to factory settings, with no prior viewing; for equipment that cannot be repaired, the storage medium (hard drive, SSD, flash memory) is removed and physically destroyed in Senegal, in Sopixcon's facilities.

No data is copied, extracted or kept, and no equipment leaves the country with data on it.

What this means in practice

  • We are approved by Senegal's Personal Data Protection Commission (CDP)
  • A data protection charter signed by all operators
  • Internal governance that guarantees confidentiality

Why it matters to you

  • Your information never circulates
  • An essential guarantee in a competitive sector
  • Confidentiality you can rely on

Proof of destruction

A unit certificate for every item

For every item, Sopixcon issues a unit certificate of wiping or destruction, linked to the serial number of the equipment and of its storage medium. It states the operation carried out, its date and the technician responsible, and can be used for your internal audit.

Frequently asked questions

Data security: your questions

Is the data on my equipment destroyed?

Yes, always. Sopixcon never accesses the content of storage media. Repairable equipment is reset to factory settings; for equipment that cannot be repaired, the storage medium is physically destroyed. No data is copied, extracted or kept.

Do I receive proof that my data has been destroyed?

Yes. A unit certificate of wiping or destruction is issued for every item, linked to its serial number.

Should I back up my data before collection?

Yes. Wiping and destruction are irreversible: no recovery is possible once an item has been taken over.

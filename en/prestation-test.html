Testing: making your quality control coverage and robustness reliable

Service line 1 — Industrial services

Test coverage assessment, bench robustness, false-negative rates, gaps against JEDEC / ISO / IPC / IEC standards.

Objective

Reducing false negatives, maximising real coverage

The Testing service line makes your equipment's entire quality control process more reliable, by pinpointing where your test benches let defects through (false negatives) and where your test coverage falls short of international standards.

Process steps

A 5-step protocol

Audit of existing test coverage

Analysis of current procedures, mapping of tested and untested areas, identification of blind spots.

Bench robustness analysis

Checking the reliability and calibration of the test equipment in place.

Bringing processes up to standard

Identification of gaps against JEDEC, ISO, IPC and IEC standards, and a compliance plan.

Setup of test benches and automated flashing

Deployment or improvement of benches, automation of flashing operations.

Calibration & coordination of certified partners

Regular calibration procedures and coordination with certified laboratories or partners where needed.

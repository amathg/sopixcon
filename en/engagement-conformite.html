Regulatory compliance

Commitment 5 / 6

WEEE accreditation from the DEEC, Basel Convention export authorisation, HS 8549 customs compliance — lawful and secure handling of your end-of-life equipment.

What this means in practice

  • WEEE accreditation issued by the DEEC (Senegal's Directorate of the Environment and Classified Establishments)
  • Export authorisations compliant with the Basel Convention
  • HS 8549 customs classification applied to all export flows

Why it matters to you

  • Lawful and secure handling of your end-of-life equipment
  • No regulatory or customs risk for your company
  • Full traceability through to final recovery

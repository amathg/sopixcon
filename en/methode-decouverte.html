Discovery: a first conversation with no commitment

Step 1 / 4 — Our method

A first discovery meeting, 1 hour, no commitment — to check that your needs and our expertise are a good fit before going further.

Objective

Checking that your needs and our expertise are a good fit

This first step helps us better understand your context and your challenges, and check that our expertise truly matches your needs — before any commitment on your part.

What this means in practice

  • Initial conversation by video call or in person
  • Presentation of your issues and challenges
  • Presentation of our approach: we diagnose, we propose, we deliver
  • Qualification of the potential scope of collaboration

Why it matters to you

  • Duration / format: 1 hour, no commitment, remotely or on site, as you prefer.
  • What you get: A joint decision on whether to launch an on-site audit, and a clear first understanding of how we work.

Preparing for the meeting

What it helps to prepare

No formal document is required. To make the hour as useful as possible, it helps to have the following in mind.

  • Your business and the nature of your equipment (telecom, IT, industrial)
  • Your main challenge: quality, costs, lead times, compliance or end of life of equipment
  • The number of sites involved and approximate volumes
  • Your regulatory, security or confidentiality constraints

Frequently asked questions

Discovery meeting: your questions

Is the discovery meeting paid?

No. The first discovery meeting is free and carries no commitment. It lasts one hour.

Where does the meeting take place?

By video call or in person, depending on your preference.

What happens after the meeting?

Together we decide whether or not to launch an on-site audit. No commercial proposal is made without this prior audit.

Ready to discuss your project?

This first meeting commits you to nothing — it's a chance to ask your questions.

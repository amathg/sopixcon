Methods: structuring your workshop's physical flow and traceability

Service line 2 — Industrial services

Reorganisation of the physical flow, unit traceability, real-time KPI dashboard and ESD compliance — up to -50% product rotations for our clients.

Objective

A better-organised workshop, flawless traceability

The Methods service line tackles the physical and information organisation of your workshop: a poorly laid-out flow and incomplete traceability lead to unnecessary rotations, lost time and quality risks.

Process steps

A 5-step protocol

Diagnosis of the current physical flow

Workshop layout analysis, cycle time measurement, bottleneck identification.

Reorganisation of the workshop layout

Optimising the arrangement of workstations to cut unnecessary movement.

Setting up unit traceability

Deployment of an automatic scanning system at every key stage of the flow.

Deployment of a real-time KPI dashboard

Continuous visibility over workshop performance for teams and management.

ESD compliance (IEC 61340)

Securing sensitive workstations against electrostatic discharge.

Typical use cases

Production or repair workshops

Reducing cycle times and unnecessary movement.

Sites with strict traceability requirements

Unit tracking required by your clients or by regulations.

ESD-sensitive environments

Protecting fragile electronic components.

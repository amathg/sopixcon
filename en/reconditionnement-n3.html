N3

Precision calibration, circuit-level repair & certification

Expert level — critical equipment and metrology requirements

Level N3 is reserved for the most demanding equipment: test and measurement instruments, high-end telecom equipment, devices subject to strict calibration requirements. It combines circuit-level repair, metrological calibration and documented final certification.

Objective

Industrial precision all the way to certification

N3 is Sopixcon's most advanced level of intervention. It is for equipment whose fault goes beyond simple component replacement and requires direct work on the tracks and printed circuit board, together with full metrological recalibration, backed by traceable certification in line with industrial standards (IPC, IEC, JEDEC depending on the equipment).

Process steps

A 5-step protocol

Circuit-level analysis

In-depth study of tracks, discrete components and interconnections to locate faults that cannot be detected at component level alone.

Printed circuit board repair

Track rebuilding, repair of cut or oxidised tracks, precision micro-soldering under the microscope to restore the circuit's electrical integrity.

Precision calibration

Adjusting the equipment to the manufacturer's specifications using calibrated reference instruments, to guarantee the expected measurement accuracy or performance.

Compliance testing against standards

Verification against applicable standards (JEDEC, IPC, IEC 61340 depending on the type of equipment) and a multi-point final check.

Final certification

Issue of a certificate of conformity and a traceable calibration report, supplied with the refurbished equipment.

Objective

A visible, well-managed supply chain

The Supply Chain service line aims to give your organisation full visibility over its supply flows, in order to reduce costs, secure stock levels and improve supplier performance.

Process steps

Flow mapping

From procurement to delivery, identifying every stage and every stakeholder.

Stock level analysis

Identifying overstock, stock-outs and their causes.

Supplier performance assessment

Analysis of your suppliers' lead times, quality and reliability.

Logistics cost optimisation

Steering & reporting tools

Setting up dashboards for continuous monitoring of the supply chain.

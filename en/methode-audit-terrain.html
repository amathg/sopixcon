On-site audit: pinpointing your needs

Step 2 / 4 — Our method

A site visit and qualifying audit over 2 to 3 weeks, to establish a factual diagnosis of your current situation.

Objective

A factual diagnosis before any proposal

No commercial proposal is made without a prior audit. This step gathers real data about your situation rather than relying on assumptions.

What this means in practice

  • Visit of your facilities and observation of flows and processes
  • Interviews with the operational teams concerned
  • Collection of factual data: current KPIs, volumes, constraints
  • Gap analysis against industrial standards

Why it matters to you

  • Duration / format: 2 to 3 weeks depending on the scope of the audit.
  • What you get: A factual, documented diagnosis of your current situation, the basis for the proposal that follows.

Preparing the visit

What makes the audit easier

The more information we have beforehand, the faster and more precise the audit. It helps to arrange:

  • A named contact and access to the sites concerned
  • Your current indicators (volumes, defect rates, lead times, stock) if they exist
  • The availability of operational teams for interviews
  • Your existing quality procedures and documents

Frequently asked questions

On-site audit: your questions

How long does an on-site audit take?

Between 2 and 3 weeks, depending on the scope of the audit (number of sites, service lines involved: testing, methods, supply chain, WEEE, refurbishment).

Is the audit mandatory before a proposal?

Yes. No commercial proposal is made without a prior audit, so that the costing and targets rest on real data.

What does the diagnosis delivered after the audit contain?

A factual, documented assessment of your situation: observed flows, collected indicators, gaps against industrial standards. It is presented at the findings review.

A situation that needs auditing?

The on-site audit is the key step in building a realistic, costed proposal.

Does Sopixcon work on one-off projects?

Sopixcon positions itself as a long-term partner, not a one-off provider. Every collaboration does begin with a first project or first flow, after an on-site audit.

Sopixcon aims to become a leading regional hub for the telecom industry and the circular economy in Africa, built on its three service areas: industrial services, WEEE recycling and refurbishment.

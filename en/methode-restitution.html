Findings review: diagnosis and costed proposal

Step 3 / 4 — Our method

Presentation of the diagnosis and a costed commercial proposal — measurable targets, not vague promises.

Objective

A clear, costed and binding proposal

This step presents the results of the on-site audit along with a detailed, costed action plan that you can approve with full knowledge of the facts.

What this means in practice

  • Detailed presentation of the diagnosis
  • Presentation of the improvement areas identified
  • Detailed action plan with priorities
  • Precise costing: costs, timelines, measurable targets

Why it matters to you

  • Duration / format: A concise presentation followed by discussion to refine the proposal if needed.
  • What you get: A clear, costed commercial proposal, committed to measurable targets.

Frequently asked questions

Findings review: your questions

What does the commercial proposal contain?

A detailed action plan with priorities, precise costing (costs and lead times) and measurable targets, drawn from the diagnosis of the on-site audit.

Are the quantified targets contractual?

Yes. They are defined with you at the findings review, built into the project contract and tracked through a KPI dashboard and periodic reporting. See our commitment to results.

Can I have the proposal adjusted?

Yes. The presentation is followed by discussion to refine the proposal if needed, before you approve it.

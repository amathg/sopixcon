Privacy Policy

How your data is handled when you contact us

This English translation is provided for convenience; in case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

Data collected

What information do we collect?

This website only collects personal data when you voluntarily fill in the contact form. The fields concerned are: full name, company (optional), email, phone (optional), type of request and message. No other personal data is collected automatically: to date, the website uses no tracking cookies or analytics tools.

Purpose of processing

Why do we collect this data?

The information submitted through the contact form is used exclusively to respond to your request (audit, testing, recycling, refurbishment or other), to get back to you and to follow up on your enquiry. It is never sold, transferred or used for third-party advertising.

Data processing & hosting

How does your data travel?

The contact form is processed by the third-party service Formspree (Formspree, Inc.), which forwards the content of your message to Sopixcon by email. Formspree acts as a technical processor for delivering these messages. To learn more about its practices, see Formspree's privacy policy.

Retention period

How long do we keep your data?

Data submitted through the form is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting business relationship, then deleted or securely archived after that period.

Your rights

Access, correction & deletion

In accordance with applicable personal data protection regulations, you have the right to access, correct and delete data concerning you. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@sopixcon.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy

For any question about this privacy policy, write to us at contact@sopixcon.com or use the website's contact form.

Key concepts

The standards in a few words

IPC

The association and reference standards for assembly, soldering and repair of electronic boards.

IEC 61340

A series of standards on controlling electrostatic phenomena, to protect components that are sensitive to discharge (ESD).

JEDEC

The standardisation body for microelectronics, whose standards are used for component reliability testing.

ISO 9001

The international quality management standard, targeted by Sopixcon within 18 months.

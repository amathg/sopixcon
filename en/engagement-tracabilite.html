Commitment

Knowing where every item is, at every moment

Unit traceability means tracking each item individually, from receipt to final destination, through a unique identifier (serial number) recorded at every step of the process.

It applies to all Sopixcon services: WEEE recycling, refurbishment and setting up traceability in your workshop.

What this means in practice

  • Every item recorded individually as soon as it is received
  • A unique identifier tracked at every stage of the process
  • A full traceability report provided to the client

Why it matters to you

  • Complete visibility over each item's journey
  • Documented proof in the event of an inspection or audit
  • Protection against any loss or mix-up between items

Frequently asked questions

Traceability: your questions

What is unit traceability?

It is the individual tracking of each item, from receipt to exit, through a unique identifier recorded at every step. It makes it possible to know the full history of an item.

Which document is given to the client?

A complete traceability report. For WEEE recycling, every item is tracked by serial number, from collection through to its final destination.

What is traceability for in case of inspection?

It provides documented proof of each item's journey, useful in case of a regulatory inspection or an internal audit.

Commitment

Measurable results, not mere promises

At Sopixcon, a project is not just a number of hours: it rests on quantified targets, defined with you at the findings review, written into the contract and tracked over time.

Examples of targets aimed for: up to +30 points of test coverage, up to -50% product rotations, up to 2x final quality.

What this means in practice

  • Quantified targets defined jointly during the findings review
  • These targets written into the project contract
  • Regular tracking through a KPI dashboard and periodic reporting

Why it matters to you

  • You know exactly what to expect
  • An objective assessment of project performance
  • A relationship based on measurable results, not mere promises

Frequently asked questions

Commitment to results: your questions

How are the quantified targets defined?

Jointly with you, at the findings review, from the diagnosis of the on-site audit. They are then built into the project contract.

How are results tracked?

Through a KPI dashboard and periodic reporting, as well as regular progress meetings with your team.

What results can be aimed for?

As examples: up to +30 points of test coverage, up to -50% product rotations and up to 2x final quality. The targets of each project depend on your starting situation.

Does this commitment meet one of your requirements?

Let's talk about your project and your specific constraints.
